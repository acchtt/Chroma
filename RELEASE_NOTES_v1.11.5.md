# Chroma v1.11.5

## Footer utility bar

- Replaces the crowded Quick Actions and About Chroma footer sections with one compact utility bar.
- Shows the Chroma name and version/runtime details on the left.
- Aligns Website, GitHub, and Check updates actions on the right with consistent sizing.
- Reduces the footer logo to 44 × 44 px and returns more vertical space to the Profiles workspace.
- Shortens the idle update action from Check for updates to Check updates.

All profile, saturation, custom-resolution, GPU-selection, updater, tray, startup, and anti-cheat behavior remains unchanged.
