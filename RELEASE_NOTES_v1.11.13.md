- Frames the Chroma logo inside a raised neon-gradient tile with a restrained glow layer.
- Changes the Chroma title to a brighter premium treatment with a short cyan-to-magenta accent line.
- Adds the slogan **Precision color, per game** in a compact gradient-outlined pill.
- Keeps the `created by acchtt` signature as a muted secondary line.
- Reworks the anti-cheat status into a balanced two-line card with an external-GPU-control descriptor.
- Restores the anti-cheat architecture disclaimer tooltip.
- Tightens Website, GitHub, and Check updates button widths and spacing while preserving their existing actions.
- Uses the dedicated footer background and stroke theme resources for improved depth in both dark and light themes.
- Rebalances the footer height, padding, divider, and control proportions as one cohesive layout.
